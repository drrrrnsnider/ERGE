## The problem

Trip planning is scattered across a dozen tabs, confirmation emails and apps.
ERGE aggregates flights, hotels, restaurants, rideshare and events into a single
itinerary.

## Who it's for

_Primary audience, their context, what they're doing when they open this._

## Principles

_Three to five. Each one should be capable of losing an argument — a principle
nothing can contradict isn't guiding anything._

## Constraints

- **Mobile-first.** Phone is the primary target; desktop is the adaptation.
- **Native wrap.** Ships to the App Store, so no server-rendered assumptions.
- **WCAG 2.2 AA**, enforced at the token layer (see `src/tokens/README.md`).
